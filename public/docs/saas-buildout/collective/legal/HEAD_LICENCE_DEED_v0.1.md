## BACKGROUND

A. The Owner owns the Body Recode intellectual property — the Layer 1 Engine and Doctrine, the Layer 2 Platform, and the Body Recode Marks (the **Body Recode IP**, as described in Schedule 1 and in the Partner IP Sublicence Deed).

B. The Company operates, or will operate, the Body Recode business and the Collective.

C. The Owner wishes to license the Body Recode IP to the Company, including the right to sublicense it to Collective Partners, while retaining full ownership. This deed records those terms.

---

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**Body Recode IP** means all Layer 1 IP, Layer 2 IP, and Body Recode Marks, as those terms are defined in the Partner IP Sublicence Deed, together with all Improvements.

**the Collective** means the programme under which the Company sublicenses a branded instance of the Platform to allied coaches. There is no limit on the number of partners. Early partners may be offered a locked-for-life founding rate, which is a pricing term only and confers no exclusivity.

**Improvements** means all improvements, modifications, extensions, and derivative works of the Body Recode IP, however arising.

**Sublicence** means a licence granted by the Company to a third party (including a Collective Partner) of rights the Company holds under this deed.

## 2. GRANT OF LICENCE

2.1 The Owner grants the Company a licence for the Term to use, reproduce, host, commercialise, and otherwise exploit the Body Recode IP for the purpose of operating the Body Recode business and the Collective, **including the right to grant Sublicences**.

2.2 **Exclusivity.** *[DECISION — LEGAL: sole/exclusive within the Body Recode field of use (recommended: the Company is the single commercial vehicle for Body Recode, while the Owner retains ownership and the right to license the IP into the Owner's other ventures and brands, e.g. Precision Coaching / Arete), OR non-exclusive. Recommend **sole/exclusive, field-limited to Body Recode**.]*

2.3 The Company may grant Sublicences only on terms no less protective of the Body Recode IP than this deed. Every Sublicence terminates automatically on termination of this deed.

## 3. OWNERSHIP RETAINED

3.1 All right, title, and interest in the Body Recode IP remains with the Owner. The Company acquires no ownership interest by this deed — only the licensed rights in clause 2.

3.2 The Company will not assign, charge, or otherwise deal with the Body Recode IP except as expressly permitted.

## 4. IMPROVEMENTS

4.1 All Improvements made by or for the Company vest in, and are assigned to, the Owner on creation, so that ownership of the Body Recode IP stays consolidated in the Owner. The Owner licenses those Improvements back to the Company under clause 2. *[DECISION — confirm this consolidation is intended.]*

## 5. CONSIDERATION

5.1 In consideration for the licence, the Company will pay the Owner a licence fee / royalty as agreed between the Parties. *[DECISION — LEGAL + ACCOUNTANT: structure and amount. Typically a licence fee or royalty from Company to Owner, arm's-length, tax-effective. Confirm transfer-pricing reasonableness and any Division 7A implications.]*

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 The Company will protect the trade secrets within the Body Recode IP (including the Engine and Doctrine) with the same rigour the Owner would, and will bind its personnel and sublicensees accordingly.

## 7. WARRANTIES

7.1 The Owner warrants that it owns the Body Recode IP and has the right to grant this licence.

7.2 To the maximum extent permitted by law, no other warranties are given. The IP is licensed on an "as is" basis between these related Parties.

## 8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 This deed commences on the date the Company is incorporated and this deed is executed, and continues while the Company operates the Body Recode business (**Term**).

8.2 The Owner may terminate this deed if the Company is subject to an insolvency event or ceases to operate the Body Recode business.

8.3 On termination: the Company's licence and all Sublicences end (subject to any partner wind-down already contracted), the Company ceases all use of the Body Recode IP, and ownership remains with the Owner throughout.

## 9. GENERAL

9.1 **Governing law.** Queensland, Australia. Exclusive jurisdiction of the Queensland courts.

9.2 **Nature.** Executed as a deed (12-year limitation period).

9.3 **Assignment.** The Company may not assign or sublicense except as expressly permitted. The Owner may deal freely with its ownership of the Body Recode IP, subject to the Company's licensed rights.

9.4 **Entire agreement, amendment, severability, counterparts.** Standard.
