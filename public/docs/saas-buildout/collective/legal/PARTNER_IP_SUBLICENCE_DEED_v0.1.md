## BACKGROUND

A. Body Recode has developed a proprietary interpretation engine (Layer 1) and coaching platform (Layer 2). Together they constitute a specialised system for reading biological, physiological, and behavioural state and delivering coaching direction.

B. Body Recode has decided to licence Layer 2 (and controlled API access to Layer 1) to a **early partners allied coaches** under the "the Collective" programme — a first, proof-of-concept group. Licensee is one of the ten. The founding programme is limited to ten partners and this licence is **non-exclusive**; Body Recode may licence the Platform to further partners after the founding programme, and Licensee's participation confers no exclusivity of any kind.

C. This deed records the intellectual property terms on which Licensee accesses and uses Body Recode's IP.

D. This deed is executed together with a Collective Partner Agreement (**Commercial Agreement**) between the Parties. Both documents form one package.

3.1 The Engine is Layer 1 IP and Excluded IP. It is not licensed for use by Licensee except as follows.

3.2 Body Recode grants Licensee a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence for the Term to access the Engine solely via the Platform's tenant-scoped API, for the purpose of receiving interpretations and coaching direction for Licensee's Partner Clients.

3.3 The Engine access licence permits Licensee to:
  (a) submit Partner Client data via the API for interpretation;
  (b) receive interpretive output and coaching direction from the Engine;
  (c) present that output to Partner Clients under Licensee's brand, subject to any coach-review-and-approve steps required by the Platform;
  (d) store Engine output as Client Data on the Instance.

3.4 The Engine access licence does not permit Licensee to:
  (a) attempt to access the Engine's underlying code, prompts, models, or Doctrine;
  (b) reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to extract or derive the algorithms, models, prompts, or Doctrine from the Engine's output;

  (d) use Engine output as a training corpus, fine-tuning corpus, or evaluation corpus for any purpose other than the ordinary review of coaching quality within Licensee's business.

3.5 Body Recode may monitor Engine usage to enforce fair use, detect anomalies, and prevent abuse.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 This deed commences on the Commencement Date under the Commercial Agreement and continues until the earlier of:

  (a) expiry or termination of the Commercial Agreement; or
  (b) termination of this deed under clause 9.2 or 9.3.

9.2 **Termination by Body Recode.** Body Recode may terminate this deed immediately by written notice if Licensee:

  (a) breaches clause 4 (Restrictions), clause 5 (Trademark and Brand), or clause 3 (Engine access limits), and does not immediately remedy the breach on written notice;
  (b) commits a material breach of any other clause and does not remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice; or
  (c) is subject to an insolvency event.

9.3 **Termination by Licensee.** Licensee may terminate this deed immediately by written notice if Body Recode:

  (a) is subject to an insolvency event; or
  (b) materially breaches its warranty in clause 8.1 and does not remedy within thirty (30) days.

9.4 Termination of this deed automatically terminates the Commercial Agreement, and vice versa.

9.5 **Effect of termination.** On termination:

  (a) all licences granted under clauses 2 and 3 end immediately, subject to any wind-down period agreed in the Commercial Agreement;
  (b) Licensee will cease all use of the Body Recode IP and Body Recode Marks;
  (c) Licensee will destroy or return any physical or electronic copies of the Body Recode IP in its possession or control (excluding archived backups where deletion is not technically feasible, which Licensee will hold subject to the confidentiality obligations in perpetuity);
  (d) accrued rights and remedies of either Party are not affected; and
  (e) provisions that are expressed to survive, or that by their nature ought to survive, continue in force.

9.6 Clauses that survive termination include: clauses 1 (Definitions), 4 (Restrictions), 5.4 and 5.5 (Restrictions on Marks + goodwill accrual), 6.1 (Licensee IP retention), 7 (Improvements), 8 (Warranties + Indemnity in respect of claims that arose before termination), 9.5 (Effect of termination), 10 (Confidentiality), 11 (Notice + General), and any other clause that by its nature is intended to survive.

### Part A: Licensed IP (Layer 2)

4. Any interpretive artefact catalog beyond what is exposed to the Instance (Foundational Reading, Program Reading, Nutrition Reading, Trajectory Reading templates and generation rules).
5. Body Recode's own client base, marketing surfaces, funnels, and content library.
6. Any code, content, or data related to other Body Recode brands (Precision Coaching, Arete Protocol, AI Coaching Movement) or other tenants.
