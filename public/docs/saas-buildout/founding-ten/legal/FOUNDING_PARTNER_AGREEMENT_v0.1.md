## BACKGROUND

A. Body Recode operates a proprietary interpretation engine and coaching platform. The engine reads a client's biological, physiological, and behavioural state and produces training, nutrition, and recovery direction. The platform sits on top of the engine and is where a coach delivers programs to clients.

B. Body Recode has decided to license the platform to a limited group of ten allied coaches under the "Founding Ten" programme. Each Founding Ten partner runs their own coaching business on a branded instance of the platform, powered by Body Recode's engine and doctrine.

C. Partner is one of the ten. Partner and Body Recode wish to record the terms on which Partner uses the platform and pays Body Recode.

D. This Agreement is accompanied by an IP Licence Deed dated the same date (**Licence Deed**). Both documents form one commercial package and must be signed together.

## AGREED TERMS

## 1. DEFINITIONS

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

**Active Client** means a coaching client on Partner's Instance who, within any calendar month, has (i) an active subscription, program, or engagement with Partner, or (ii) submitted a Weekly Check-In, intake, or other tenant-scoped record.

**Business Day** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in Brisbane, Queensland.

**Client Data** means all data relating to Partner's clients that is stored on, generated by, or processed through Partner's Instance.

**Commencement Date** means the date this Agreement is signed by both Parties, or such other date as recorded in Item 1 of Schedule 1.

**Doctrine** means the interpretation rules, safety floors, and clinical logic encoded in the Engine and its supporting documentation, as maintained and updated by Body Recode.

**Doctrine Mode A** means the operating mode where the Engine runs Body Recode's Doctrine without modification, branded under Partner's brand. Doctrine Mode A applies to all Founding Ten partners unless expressly changed in writing.

**Engine** means Body Recode's interpretation engine, comprising the algorithms, prompts, machine learning models, and Doctrine used to read a client's state and produce coaching direction. The Engine is Layer 1 IP under the Licence Deed and is not licensed except by API access under this Agreement.

**Fees** means the setup fee, subscription fee, and per-active-client fee set out in Schedule 1.

**Founding Ten Discount** means the reduced setup fee and locked subscription rate available to the first ten Founding Ten partners, as recorded in Schedule 1.

**Hard Safety Floors** means the safety limits set out in Schedule 3 including but not limited to RRS clamps, Fat Map training limits, injury contraindications, and eligibility floors. Hard Safety Floors cannot be overridden by Partner.

**Instance** means the tenant-scoped instance of the Platform provisioned for Partner, including Partner's tenant configuration, branding, domain routing, and Client Data.

**Layer 1 IP** has the meaning given in the Licence Deed. In summary, it is the Engine and Doctrine.

**Layer 2 IP** has the meaning given in the Licence Deed. In summary, it is the coaching web application and its supporting infrastructure.

**Modality** means the training discipline within which Partner delivers coaching, being one of the Modalities defined in Schedule 4 (currently: strength / performance coaching, or yoga / movement).

**Partner Client** means a natural person who has entered into a coaching arrangement with Partner (whether paid or free) and interacts with Partner via the Instance.

**Platform** means the coaching web application operated by Body Recode, comprising Layer 2 IP and providing tenant-scoped access to the Engine via the API.

**Powered by Body Recode Mark** means the trade mark and lockup set out in Schedule 2 of the Licence Deed, use of which is optional for Partner.

**Term** has the meaning given in clause 4.

## 14. TERMINATION

14.1 **Termination by Body Recode for cause.** Body Recode may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if:
  (a) Partner materially breaches this Agreement and does not remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice;
  (b) Partner is subject to an insolvency event, including administration, receivership, liquidation, or the Personal Insolvency Agreement equivalent;
  (c) Partner uses the Instance to breach or circumvent the Hard Safety Floors, and does not immediately cease on written notice;
  (d) Partner is found to have committed misconduct that materially damages Body Recode's reputation; or
  (e) Partner is subject to a suspension or cancellation of a required professional registration or qualification.

14.2 **Termination by Partner for cause.** Partner may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if:
  (a) Body Recode materially breaches this Agreement and does not remedy the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice;
  (b) Body Recode is subject to an insolvency event; or
  (c) Body Recode ceases to operate the Platform.

14.3 **Termination for convenience.** After the Initial Term, either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice.

14.4 **Effect of termination.** On termination:
  (a) Partner's licence to use Layer 2 IP and the Engine (under the Licence Deed) ends;
  (b) Partner's access to the Instance is suspended after a wind-down period (see clause 15);
  (c) Body Recode will invoice, and Partner will pay, all Fees accrued to the effective date of termination;
  (d) any non-refundable amounts previously paid remain non-refundable;
  (e) if Body Recode has been terminated for cause by Partner, Body Recode will refund the pro-rata unused portion of the setup fee for the balance of the Initial Term; and
  (f) provisions that are expressed to survive, or that by their nature ought to survive, continue in force.

## 15. WIND-DOWN AND CLIENT HANDOVER

15.1 Body Recode will provide Partner with a wind-down period of forty-five (45) days from the effective date of termination (**Wind-Down Period**) during which Partner may:
  (a) continue to access the Instance in a read-only mode;
  (b) export Client Data in a machine-readable format (JSON, CSV);
  (c) communicate the transition to Partner Clients;
  (d) transition Partner Clients to another platform or continue coaching them offline; and
  (e) settle any outstanding Fees.

15.2 During the Wind-Down Period, Body Recode may charge a wind-down subscription equal to fifty percent (50%) of the previously-applicable monthly subscription fee.

15.3 At the end of the Wind-Down Period, Body Recode will:
  (a) delete or return all Client Data as directed by Partner;
  (b) delete or de-provision the Instance; and
  (c) revoke all Partner and Sub-User credentials.

15.4 Body Recode will retain aggregated, de-identified operational data indefinitely for its own analytics and Doctrine tuning, consistent with clause 9.4.

15.5 Body Recode will not, without Partner's prior written consent, directly market Body Recode's own coaching services to Partner Clients whose contact details Body Recode came to hold only as a result of processing Client Data. This restriction applies for twelve (12) months after termination.

## SCHEDULE 3 — HARD SAFETY FLOORS

The following limits are enforced by the Engine and cannot be overridden by Partner:

1. **RRS (Recovery Regulation Signal) clamps** — session load and RRS scores below Engine-defined thresholds trigger mandatory deload or rest recommendations that cannot be bypassed.

2. **Fat Map training limits** — training volume and intensity ceilings based on the client's Fat Map zone.

3. **Injury contraindications** — exercise substitutions and program modifications required by declared injuries, per the Injury Substitution Matrix.

4. **Eligibility floors** — clients who fail the Lead Quality Filter or who present with disclosed contraindications (medication interactions, medical conditions) are gated from certain coaching pathways.

5. **Nutrition safety floors** — minimum calorie floors, protein floors, and appetite-suppression rule sets that apply automatically.

6. **Doctrine language filters** — banned client-facing terminology and doctrine version enforcement.

Body Recode reserves the right to update the Hard Safety Floors on notice to Partner. Updates that materially change Partner's obligations require at least thirty (30) days' notice.
