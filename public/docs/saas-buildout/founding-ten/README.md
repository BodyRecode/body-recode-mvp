# 00_FOUNDING_TEN — The direction

This folder holds the current strategic core of Studio of Ten: the **Founding Ten powered-platform program**. Read this first.

Last updated: 2026-06-30

---

## What SOT is now

Studio of Ten has pulled back from leading with bespoke "we'll build you anything" studio work (call that **Lane A**). The front door is now the **powered platform** (**Lane B**):

> A practitioner gets the **Body Recode coaching engine + website + onboarding**, white-labelled to their brand and their modality, delivered as a productised package. Capped at **ten founding partners**.

Lane A still exists as bespoke overflow, but it is no longer the pitch. The first ten seats prove the powered model; evergreen comes after.

## Why "ten"

The cap is the offer. Ten lets each founding partner get real attention during build + launch, keeps the platform multi-tenant-but-curated, and creates honest scarcity for the founding pricing. After the ten are live and stable, the model opens up (B: method-injection, evergreen seats).

## The two layers we sell

- **Layer 1 — the interpretation engine.** Diagnosis: scorecard → reading → state. Runs the BR doctrine now (decision **A**); partners inject their own method later (**B**).
- **Layer 2 — the coaching application.** Prescription: programs, nutrition, check-ins, portal. Everything coach-gated. The scorecard lead magnet sits at the top of Layer 2.
