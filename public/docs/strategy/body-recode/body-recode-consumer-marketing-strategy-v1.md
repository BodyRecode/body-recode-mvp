# Body Recode - Consumer Marketing Strategy

**Status:** v1.0 - 2026-07-19. Consolidated from the locked strategy, the live funnel, and the positioning bank.
**Scope:** the **consumer demand brand** - `@body_recode_`, bodyrecode.au marketing, and the scorecard -> Challenge -> Blueprint -> coaching/membership funnel. This is Body Recode's *consumer* face. It does **not** cover the licensing engine (see the Collective strategy) or the internal coaching delivery (Performance Coaching).

---

## 1. The one idea

**Interpretation before prescription.** Most people are told what to do before anyone has read what their body is actually doing. Body Recode reads the body first - names the **state** it is operating in - and only then prescribes. Every piece of content, every ad, every funnel step bends back to that one idea.

The consumer promise: *"You are doing the work and getting nothing back. That is not a discipline problem. It is a body-state problem - and it is readable."*

---

## 2. Who we talk to

High-functioning adults whose bodies have stopped responding to effort - training hard, eating well, consistent, and getting nothing back. Four archetypes, **skewed toward the Depleted** end (bodies in protection/remediation, not the lean-and-optimising end).

The emotional truth we lead with (cold audience): *"You're training. You're eating well. You're consistent. And nothing is moving."* Name the feeling before any brand vocabulary. Never open cold content with doctrine words - lead with what they feel, then reveal the read underneath it.

---

## 3. The body-state doctrine (the spine of everything)

The body operates in one of three states, and each needs a different approach - the same program that works in one state makes things worse in another:

- **Depleted / Remediation** - protection mode; more training + less food makes it worse.
- **Transitioning / Optimisation** - responding, needs directed load.
- **Ready / Post-Optimisation** - can push.

Supporting assets that carry this: the **Body State Scorecard** (names state + pattern with a confidence tier), the **Fat Map**, the **Six Pillars**, and the canonical patterns (Stress-Stored / Insulin-Drift / Estrogen-Shift / Androgen-Decline). The arc verbs are **Decode -> Rewire -> Rebuild** (baked into the product names; never invent alternates).

---

## 4. The funnel (scorecard-first)

Everything routes to the **Body State Scorecard** first - it is the free, no-email-until-result diagnostic that turns a stranger into a named, segmented lead. The ladder from there:

| Stage | What it is | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Body State Scorecard** | Free 2-minute diagnostic. Names your state + pattern. The universal front door. | Free |
| **14-Day Body Decode Challenge** | Free 14-day challenge (Funnel B). Day-0 intake, in-portal experience, Day-14 reveal. Proof that the read works. | Free |
| **The Blueprint** | The paid next step after the Challenge - your personalised plan off the read. | $97 |
| **Coaching / Membership** | Ongoing Performance Coaching + membership - the delivery arm the funnel feeds. | Paid tiers |

Two ad streams feed the top: **cold traffic -> Scorecard** (diagnosis-led), and **retargeting -> Challenge + Membership**. Local/warm can route straight to the Challenge. Promos always route to `/scorecard?intent=challenge`, never straight to a sales page.

**Rule:** the scorecard is the hub. Reach is upstream of conversion - before blaming a caption or CTA, find which gate the post failed at and fix the earliest broken step.

---

## 5. Channels & cadence

- **Instagram `@body_recode_` - primary.** ~5x per week. Feed posts (authority, pattern, contrarian, coach, diagnostic), carousels for frameworks, and Stories. This is the demand engine.
- **Paid (Meta) - cold statics -> Scorecard.** Cold ad sets are statics only (no reels/video), optimised for the `Lead` event. Budget ramps deliberately (see the Meta budget plan) - never scale before the funnel converts.
- **Bio / links -> Scorecard.** bodyrecode.au/links is the IG-bio target; every path leads to the diagnostic.
- **Email** - post-scorecard nurture, Challenge sequence, Blueprint CTA, weekly check-ins.

Consumer content is state-first: reels, captions, ads, emails, landing pages, even error states all bend back to state-first language.

---

## 6. Content pillars (consumer)

Five recurring themes; every consumer post is one of these:

1. **Name the Feeling** - the stuck experience (doing everything, nothing moving). Cold-open empathy before doctrine.
2. **Read Before Rx** - interpretation before prescription; why generic programs fail because they skip the read.
3. **The Three States** - Depleted / Transitioning / Ready; why the same effort produces opposite results.
4. **Proof & Pattern** - client trajectories, the named patterns, scorecard results, the Challenge in action.
5. **Take the Scorecard** - the diagnostic CTA. Two minutes, no email until the result. Every one of these ends at the Scorecard.

---

## 7. Voice & design rules

- **Voice:** interpretation-led, calm authority, no hype. Contractions, varied rhythm, read-aloud test. Fat-loss-explicit framing. No "tips" content - always the read underneath.
- **No em dashes** (house style) - hyphens or rewrites.
- **Design language:** Pure White / Graphite / Signal Blue (#1B6DFC). Social assets 1080x1350 (4:5); reels 9:16; 96px safe margins. All photos of Kade in Body Recode graphics render **greyscale**, never colour.
- Kade's public title in bylines: **"Sports Scientist"** (not "Exercise Scientist").

---

## 8. Messaging rules (do / don't)

**Do:** lead with the stuck feeling; name the state; make the Scorecard the destination; use proof (real trajectories, with consent); keep the read ahead of the prescription.

**Don't:** open cold with doctrine vocabulary; sell the Blueprint or coaching before the Scorecard; run reels/video in cold-paid ad sets; use colour photos of Kade in BR graphics; promise outcomes without the state read behind them; blur into the coach-licensing (Collective) or B2B audience - this brand talks to end-clients only.

---

## 9. What we measure

- **Scorecard completions / week** (true top of funnel).
- **Scorecard -> Challenge enrolment rate.**
- **Challenge -> Blueprint conversion.**
- **Cost per Lead** (Meta, the only event the site fires) and blended CAC.
- Reach -> save -> profile-visit -> scorecard, gate by gate, to find the earliest broken step.

---

## 10. How this brand relates to the others

- **Body Recode (this doc)** = consumer demand. Fills the top of the funnel and feeds coaching.
- **Performance Coaching** = the delivery arm the funnel feeds (not separately marketed).
- **The Collective** = the B2B licensing engine ("Powered by Body Recode"). Different audience (coaches), different channel (LinkedIn). Never mixed into consumer content.
- **Personal brand (`@kade_dunstone_`)** = Kade's first-person voice; sends warm audience toward Body Recode but keeps its own pillars.
