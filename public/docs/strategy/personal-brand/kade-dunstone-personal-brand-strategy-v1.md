## BRAND 1 — KADE DUNSTONE (PERSONAL)

### Positioning

Kade Dunstone is a performance coach, systems thinker, and software builder who has rebuilt his identity, career, and direction multiple times - and built frameworks out of every rebuild.

He is not a fitness influencer.
He is not a motivational speaker.
He is not a generic founder content creator.

He is someone who learned to interpret before acting - and built systems around that principle across the body, business, and software.

The platform is the proof. Most coaches rent software. Kade built his own - a full coaching and CRM platform - using the same AI co-founder principles he teaches.

### The Three Rebuilds (Core Story)

Every piece of content connects back to one of these three moments.

**Rebuild 1 — Military discharge**
Built toward soldier from childhood. Medically discharged due to structural leg issues. Lost the identity he had constructed for years.
What it produced: A deep understanding of the body, physical limitation, and how structure creates stability when direction is lost.

**Rebuild 2 — Commercial fitness exit**
Helped build and operate high-performing gym environments across Australia. Chose to step away due to misalignment — not lack of capability.
What it produced: The understanding that scale without systems creates noise. Systems create leverage. Sessions are not a business.

**Rebuild 3 — 20-year relationship end**
Walked away from a shared life and business. Rebuilt self-trust and clarity from scratch.
What it produced: The realisation that people don't lose performance — they lose clarity. Clarity is upstream of everything.

**The Build — Body Recode Platform**
After three rebuilds, stopped renting other people's software and built his own.
A full coaching and CRM platform - upstream client assessment, downstream performance coaching operations, full business dashboard - built using AI as a co-founder.
What it produced: Proof that the principles are real. If you can interpret, structure, and execute - you can build anything.

### Core Philosophy

Interpret before you act.

Without correct interpretation — effort is misdirected, progress stalls, burnout increases.
With correct interpretation — decisions become clear, execution becomes effective, results become repeatable.

This applies to the body. To business. To identity.

### Contrarian Positions (Content Foundation)

These are the beliefs that drive all content. Each is a post, a thread, a video.

1. Most coaches prescribe without interpreting first
2. Effort is not the problem. Misdirected effort is.
3. You cannot out-train a nervous system in protection mode
4. Most people don't have a discipline problem. They have a clarity problem.
5. Systems scale. Hustle doesn't.
6. Rebuilding is not failure. It is the process of building better structure.
7. The body is not broken. It is being misread.
8. AI does not create value. It distributes structured value. If your thinking isn't structured, AI amplifies noise.
9. Most coaching businesses deliver sessions. They should be delivering systems.
10. Identity determines direction. Get the identity wrong and the strategy never lands.
11. Most coaches rent software built by someone else. Building your own forces you to understand your business at a different level.
12. You don't need to know how to code. You need to know what you're building and why. AI handles the rest.

## THE ECOSYSTEM LOGIC

### How the three brands work together

1. Someone discovers @kade_dunstone_ through a post about performance, identity, or AI
2. They follow because the thinking resonates
3. Over time they encounter Body Recode through performance content
4. Or they encounter AI Co-Founder Method through AI and leverage content
5. They buy or apply based on which problem is most relevant to them right now
6. One person can buy both products at different points
